Maren House

A residential interior-design studio with beautiful projects and a quiet feed. We rebuilt the account into an editorial presence that looks like the rooms themselves.

The brief

The work was there — considered, warm, genuinely lovely rooms — but the feed didn't show it. Posts were sporadic, off-brand, and the studio had no time to fix it. They needed a presence that matched the quality of the projects, without adding to their week.

What I did

We set a clear strategy first: who the studio wanted to reach and the three content pillars that would speak to them. From there I built an editorial content system — carousels and posts drawn from existing project photography, captions in the studio's voice, and a steady weekly cadence handled end to end.
