About

The studio behind
the studios.

Placeholder — portrait of Aleksandra

I'm Aleksandra, founder of AN in the Studios. I started this studio because I kept seeing beautiful interior work go unseen — brilliant designers too busy building rooms to build an audience.

My background sits at an unusual intersection: project management and design. The PM side means things get delivered, on time, without chasing. The design side means the work actually looks the way your studio deserves. Warm, editorial, considered.

I work with a small number of interior-design studios at a time, so each one gets real attention. If that sounds like what you've been missing, I'd love to hear about your work.

What I believe

A few quiet principles.

  • Consistency beats intensity. A calm feed every week wins.
  • Your work is the hero. My job is to frame it, not shout over it.
  • No jargon, no games. Clear work, clearly delivered.
