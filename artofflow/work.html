Selected work

A manufacturer
that stopped chasing.

A European window manufacturer, exporting to the United States, where the admin around every order had quietly become a second job.

European manufacturer · anonymous
SectorWindow manufacturing
MarketsEurope → United States
EngagementDesign, build & care

The brief

Exporting across the Atlantic meant every enquiry carried a long tail of work: quotes to prepare across time zones, specifications to translate between standards, documents to assemble for shipping, and follow-ups that depended on one or two people remembering. The product was excellent and demand was there — but the team was spending its days on coordination instead of customers, and things occasionally slipped.

What we built

We started with a call and a week of watching how the work really moved — not how the org chart said it did. Then we designed a small set of systems around the team's existing tools, so nothing had to be ripped out or relearned.

Incoming enquiries are now captured, sorted and answered quickly, with the right specification and context already attached. The repetitive paperwork that sits between a confirmed order and a shipment is assembled automatically and checked by a person before it goes. Follow-ups happen on their own schedule rather than from memory. And the team has an assistant that knows the company's products and processes, so answers that used to mean interrupting a colleague now take seconds.

The result

The coordination that used to fill the day now happens in the background. Enquiries get answered faster, the export paperwork is consistent, and nothing depends on a single person holding it all in their head. The team spends its time with customers and on the product — and the work keeps moving when the office is closed.

Client details are kept anonymous at their request.
