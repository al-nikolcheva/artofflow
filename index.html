What we build

Systems for the work that repeats.

Most businesses lose hours to the same jobs every week. We build AI into those jobs, inside the tools you already use, so they happen on their own.

I

Clients

From first message to happy client.

When someone gets in touch, they hear back within minutes, at any hour, in your tone of voice. Their questions are answered, the right details are collected, and the enquiry lands with the right person, ready to act on.

After the work is done, clients are thanked, asked for feedback and reminded when it is time to come back. Nobody has to remember to do it.

For exampleA message arrives on a Sunday evening. By Monday morning it has a reply, a short summary and a suggested next step waiting for you.
II

Sales

Fewer leads lost in the gaps.

Quotes and proposals are drafted from your own prices and past jobs, so your team checks and sends them rather than writing from scratch.

Every open lead gets a follow-up at the right moment, and you can see at a glance who is waiting on whom.

For exampleA request for a quote comes in with drawings attached. A first draft, with the right products and notes, is ready for review the same day.
III

Marketing

Show up consistently, without the scramble.

A steady rhythm of posts, newsletters and campaigns, written in your voice and built from what is actually happening in your business: new projects, client stories, seasonal moments.

You approve. It goes out. You hear what worked.

For exampleA finished project becomes a short case story, a social post and a line in next month’s newsletter, ready for your sign-off.
IV

Operations

The back office, handled.

Bookings, invoices, order updates, weekly reports, handovers between teams. We connect the steps so information moves on its own and people only step in where judgement is needed.

You get a clear picture of the week without asking anyone to put it together.

For exampleEvery Friday, a one-page summary of new work, open jobs and anything overdue arrives in your inbox.
V

An AI assistant for your team

Everything your business knows, one question away.

A private assistant that has read your documents, products, prices and processes. Anyone on the team can ask it a question and get an answer based on your own information, with the source to check.

New starters get up to speed faster, and your experienced people stop answering the same questions twice.

For example“What finish options do we offer on this range, and what is the lead time?” Answered in seconds, from your own product sheets.
How we work

Call. Design. Build & run.

A calm, clear process. You always know what we are doing, why, and what it will change for you.

01

Call

Thirty minutes, no preparation needed.

We talk about how your business runs today: where enquiries come from, what your team repeats every week, and what you would do with the time back.

  • No pitch and no jargon
  • An honest view on whether AI will help
  • If it will, a clear next step
02

Design

A plan you can read in ten minutes.

We look closely at the jobs you want to hand over and map how they should run. You get a short written plan: what we will build, which tools it uses, what changes for your team, and how we will know it works.

  • Built around the tools you already use
  • Your approval before anything is built
03

Build & run

Made, tested, handed over.

We build the system, test it with real work and train your team to use it. Then we switch it on and watch it closely in the first weeks.

  • Your data stays in your accounts
  • A person checks anything that matters before it goes out
  • Simple written guides for your team
+

Care

We stay after launch.

Businesses change, and so do the tools. We look after what we build: we keep it running, adjust it as you grow, and suggest the next thing worth handing over when you are ready.

After launch

A loop, not a finish line.

Once a system is live, we keep an eye on it. We see what it handles well and where it needs a person, then adjust it. Each round, it fits your business a little better.

VALDA. A window maker, selling across an ocean.

Marketing, Sales

VALDA makes high-end windows and doors in Europe and sells them in the United States. The product was excellent. Being seen in the US, and turning that attention into sales, was the hard part. We solved both.

ClientVALDA
MakesWindows and doors
MarketUnited States
SolvedMarketing and Sales
Two problems, both solved

Seen by the right people in the US.

The problem

Posting happened by hand, when someone had time. There was no steady presence in the US, and no clear view of what was working.

What we built

A monthly content loop. Posts and short videos are made, planned on a calendar, approved by a person and published on a steady rhythm. Each month, what worked shapes what comes next.

The result

VALDA now shows up consistently, in its own voice, for the audience that matters, and the plan gets better every month.

Interest turned into conversations.

The problem

Finding new US leads depended on spare time, and with the time difference, people who showed interest could go unanswered.

What we built

A warm-lead flow. The right prospects are found and engaged with genuinely. Anyone who shows interest gets a personal message, and when they ask for a quote, a person takes over.

The result

A steady flow of warm US leads. Every interested person hears back, and the team spends its time on real conversations.

Who does what

Across both

The system

  • Makes, plans and schedules content
  • Finds and engages warm prospects
  • Sends the first personal message
  • Checks what worked

The team

  • Supplies the photos
  • Approves everything that goes out
  • Replies and answers every lead

A summary only. The full design of both systems stays with VALDA. We do not publish client figures, and we are happy to talk this project through on a call.

About us

A small studio for considered businesses.

Art of Flow is a London studio. We build AI systems for businesses that care about how things are done.

The studio began with a simple observation. The best businesses are often held back by the quiet, repetitive work behind every sale and every client: the replies, the chasing, the copying from one place to another. None of it is difficult. All of it takes time.

AI can now do much of that work well, but only when it is shaped around the way a particular business runs. Off-the-shelf tools rarely fit. So we design each system by hand, around your people, your tools and your standards.

We work with a small number of clients at a time. We explain things in plain words, we build carefully, and we stay to look after what we make.

How we see it

AI for the repeat work. People for the judgement.

What we hold to
Plain words

If we cannot explain it simply, we have not understood it yet.

People first

Systems take the repetitive work. People keep the judgement and the relationships.

Quietly reliable

The best system is the one you stop noticing because it simply works.
