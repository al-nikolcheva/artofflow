No. 03How we workArt of Flow · London

Slow to start, so it runs smoothly for years.

Three steps, done with you rather than to you. Your team keeps working as normal while we build in the background.

01Audit

We start by listening.

Over one or two sessions we sit with you and your team and follow a normal week. Where do enquiries come in? What gets copied from one place to another? What is always a little late? You receive a short written report with the five or six changes that would give you the most time back, and an honest view of what is not worth automating.

02Design

A plan you can read in ten minutes.

We sketch how each new flow will work, in plain words and simple drawings, using the tools you already pay for wherever we can. You see exactly what will happen, who stays in control, and what it will cost before anything is built.

03Build and run

Built carefully, handed over gently.

We build in small steps and test each one with your team on real work. Nothing goes live until you are happy with it. We then show everyone how it works, write a one-page guide, and stay close for the first weeks.

04Care

Looked after, every month.

Systems need tending, like a garden. Care is our monthly retainer, so everything keeps working as your business grows.

05Questions

Often asked.

How long does it take?

The audit takes about two weeks. Most first systems are built and running within four to six weeks after that. Larger pieces are planned in stages, so you see results early.

What tools do you use?

Wherever possible, the ones you already have, such as Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, your email, calendar and accounts software. Where something new helps, we choose well-known, reliable tools and explain why.

Will my staff need training?

A little, and we do it with them. Each system comes with a short walkthrough and a one-page guide. Most people find their day simply gets lighter, rather than more complicated.

What about data privacy?

We work under UK GDPR. We only use the data a system needs, keep it inside accounts you own, and document where it goes. AI tools are set up so your information is not used to train public models. We are happy to sign a data processing agreement.

What does it cost?

Every business is different, so we price after the audit. You receive a fixed price for the build and a clear monthly fee for Care. No surprises.
