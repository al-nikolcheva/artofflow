Privacy

Last updated — draft, review before launch

This is a simple, plain-language note about how I handle the information you share with me. It's a starting point — have it checked against UK GDPR before you publish.

What I collect

When you use the contact form or email me, I receive the details you choose to send: your name, email address, your studio or Instagram handle, and your message. I don't collect anything you don't give me directly.

How I use it

I use your details only to reply to you and to discuss working together. I don't sell your information, and I don't add you to any marketing list without asking first.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are handled by a form service and delivered to my email inbox. Those providers process your data on my behalf; each has its own privacy terms. Update this section with the specific services you use before launch.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to see what I hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Just email hello@aninthestudios.com and I'll take care of it.

Contact

Questions about any of this? Email hello@aninthestudios.com.
