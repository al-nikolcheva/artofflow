Cookies

Last updated: November 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. We keep their use to a minimum.

Necessary

The site remembers your cookie choice in your browser so we don’t ask again. This is needed for the site to work and can’t be switched off here.

Optional: measuring visits

If you click “Accept”, we may use simple analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. We never use advertising or tracking cookies. If you click “Decline”, none are set.

Changing your mind

You can clear cookies in your browser settings at any time, or reset your choice here, and we will ask again on your next visit.
