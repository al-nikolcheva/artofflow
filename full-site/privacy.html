Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Please review with a professional before launch.

This policy explains how Art of Flow (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information through artofflow.studio, in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Art of Flow is based in London. For anything about your data, email hello@artofflow.studio. [Add your registered business name and address.]

What we collect

  • What you send us through the contact form: your name, company, email address and message.
  • Details you share when booking a call, through our booking provider.
  • If you accept optional cookies, anonymous information about how the site is used.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange calls (legitimate interests, or steps before a contract).
  • To deliver work you have asked us to do (contract).
  • To improve the website, only with your consent.

We never sell your information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only trusted providers who help us run the business, such as our email, form and booking providers [list them, e.g. Microsoft 365, Formspree, Cal.com]. Some may process data outside the UK under approved safeguards.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work are deleted after 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as UK law requires, usually six years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to or limit how we use it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Email us and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
