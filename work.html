Selected work.

Each project starts with a real problem and ends with a quieter week. Here is the first, with more to follow.

Client
London service business
Team
Small, owner-led
Areas
Sales, Clients, Marketing
Status
Running, with monthly Care
01The situation

Good work, no time to find more of it.

The owner was doing the work, answering every email and trying to find new clients in the evenings. Leads lived in a spreadsheet, follow-ups depended on memory, and social posts went out when there was a spare hour, which was rarely.

02What we built

Five small systems, working together.

  • Lead generation. A weekly list of well-matched businesses, checked and ready to contact.
  • A simple client record (CRM). One place for every lead, client and conversation.
  • Email outreach. Personal first emails and gentle follow-ups, sent in the owner’s voice.
  • Social posting. Posts prepared and scheduled each week from the studio’s own work.
  • Analysis. A short monthly summary of what is bringing in interest.
03How it runs now

Quietly, in the background.

New leads arrive each Monday. Follow-ups go out on their own and replies land in one inbox. Posts are ready a week ahead. The owner spends a short half-hour each week reviewing, and the rest of the time on clients.
