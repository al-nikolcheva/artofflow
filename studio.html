01Why Art of Flow

Good businesses shouldn’t run on late nights.

Art of Flow began with a simple observation: the people doing the best work are often the ones buried in admin. Not because they are disorganised, but because no one has had the time to set things up properly.

Technology can help, but most of it is sold loudly and set up badly. We wanted the opposite: a few well-made systems, built with care, that quietly do their job.
